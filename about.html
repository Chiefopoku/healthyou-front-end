About

Small, consistent actions add up

HealthYou makes healthy living simple: a few well-timed reminders and practical tips, without the clutter.

The story

HealthYou grew out of a personal problem: on long, busy days it is easy to forget to drink water, take vitamins, or simply stand up and stretch. I wanted a simple, friendly tool that helps with exactly those small things.

It began as my portfolio project at Holberton School. This site is the front-end; a full-stack version with a Python/Flask back-end and database is in the healthyou_ repository.

Our mission

To make healthy living simple and achievable for everyone, one small habit at a time.

Emmanuel Opoku-Adjei

Designer and developer. Registered nurse turned laboratory scientist.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is HealthYou?

A web app that helps you keep up daily health routines with reminders, tips and a BMI calculator.

How do I set a reminder?

Open the Dashboard, choose a reminder type and how often, then select “Add reminder”. Allow notifications when your browser asks.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server, and you can export or delete it any time from the Account page.

Is HealthYou free?

Yes. It is a free demo project.

Is this medical advice?

No. Tips are general wellness information. Always follow advice from your own clinician.
